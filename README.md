# The Rounds

**The Rounds** is a flashcard-style study binder built for nursing students — a place to turn lecture notes into atomic, quizzable flashcards, organize them the way a real study binder is organized, and share them with a clinical group without losing control of what you wrote.

Nursing school throws a lot at once — fundamentals, pharmacology, med-surg, OB & peds, psych, critical care — and most of what students remember comes from actively recalling material, not re-reading long notes. The Rounds is built around that: every entry is a small, focused flashcard (a term and its description), organized by topic and folder, and reviewable in a quiz that makes you type the answer instead of just rereading it.

## What it does

- **A personal binder, organized in layers.** Every student gets a private space where notes are flashcards, filed under Topic → Folder → Flashcard (e.g. Pharmacology → Prelims → "Furosemide"). Folders and topics are created automatically just by typing a name — no separate setup screen.
- **One flashcard, one idea.** Each entry has a title, a focus term (the thing to memorize), and a description (its explanation) — deliberately atomic, so a big topic becomes many small cards instead of one long note.
- **Import flashcards from your own lecture material.** Copy a ready-made prompt, paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, or another LLM along with your lecture PDF, and upload the markdown file it gives back. The app parses it straight into flashcards in whichever folder you're working in.
- **Duplicate-aware.** Whether you're importing a batch or just checking your existing binder, The Rounds flags flashcards that cover the same term — across your own notes *and* everything shared with you in a group — so you can review and clean up instead of ending up with silent copies.
- **Quiz mode.** Pick which topics or folders to include and how many cards you want, then get quizzed: you're shown the description and have to type the term. Answers are graded leniently (case and spacing don't matter), scored, and reviewable afterward card-by-card.
- **Study groups.** Find classmates and form a group — a clinical group, a study group, a whole cohort. Groups show a synchronized, organized feed of every flashcard shared into them, browsable by topic and folder just like a personal binder, with quiz mode available on the shared set too.
- **Contribute without losing control.** Any flashcard — or a whole folder, topic, or your entire binder at once — can be shared into a group in one action. Everyone in the group can read it, but only the person who wrote it can edit, move, or delete it. Sharing never means giving up ownership.
- **Built for actually using it on the fly.** Multi-select flashcards to bulk move, share, or delete them; drag a card onto a folder in the sidebar to refile it; everything scales down to a phone-sized screen without losing the desktop layout's functionality.
- **Sign in with Google, nothing else.** No separate password to create or leak. New accounts get a quick, optional prompt to set a nickname and a picture — upload one or just pick from a set of preset icons.

## Why it's organized this way

Nursing students already think in rotations and terms, not paragraphs — it's how coursework, clinicals, and exams are structured, and it's how active recall actually works. The Rounds mirrors that instead of asking students to invent their own filing system or write long-form notes they'll never re-read. And because every flashcard is personally owned even inside a shared group, students can pool knowledge with classmates without worrying about someone overwriting or deleting something they wrote.

## Status

The Rounds is live and in active use — Google sign-in, personal binders, group sharing, `.md` import, duplicate checking, and quiz mode are all built and deployed. Development continues based on real feedback from the students using it.
