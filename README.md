# The Rounds

**The Rounds** is a study binder built for nursing students — a place to write, organize, and share clinical notes the way a real chart binder is organized: by rotation.

Nursing school throws a lot at once — fundamentals, pharmacology, med-surg, OB & peds, psych, critical care — and most of what students remember comes from notes they wrote themselves. The Rounds gives those notes a home that's actually organized around how nursing programs are structured, instead of a flat pile of documents or scattered messaging-app threads.

## What it does

- **A personal binder.** Every student gets their own private space to write and organize notes by rotation, tag them, and search across everything they've written.
- **Structured notes, not just text.** Notes support headings, bullet lists, and bold text, so a single entry can hold an entire lecture's worth of organized material — not just a flat paragraph.
- **Study groups.** Find classmates and form a group — a clinical group, a study group, a whole cohort. Groups share a synchronized set of notes.
- **Contribute without losing control.** Any note can be shared into a group with one toggle. Everyone in the group can read it, but only the person who wrote it can edit or delete it. Sharing a note never means giving up control of it.
- **Sign in with Google, nothing else.** No separate password to create or leak — one less thing for a student juggling clinicals to worry about.

## Why it's organized this way

Nursing students already think in rotations — it's how coursework, clinicals, and exams are structured. The Rounds mirrors that instead of asking students to invent their own filing system from scratch. And because notes are personally owned even inside a shared group, students can pool knowledge with classmates without worrying about someone overwriting or deleting something they wrote.
